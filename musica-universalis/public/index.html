Welcome to Musica Universalis. Long ago, thinkers imagined that the universe held a hidden harmony: musica universalis, the music of the spheres. Today, we use that poetic idea to listen to something real. NASA visualizations reveal patterns of particles moving through Earth's atmosphere: dust, sea salt, smoke and sulfates. We assign each a musical voice, so invisible movements become an experience you can hear. This is sonification, not a recording of the particles themselves. Are you ready to listen to Earth?
